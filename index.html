Who We Are

Navigating Challenges, Engineering Success

Red Hawk Investment is a premier Omani contracting and engineering firm headquartered in Sohar. We provide high-quality structural steel fabrication, offshore construction, and specialized technical staffing solutions to the Middle East's energy and marine industries.

Our foundations are built on solid ethical principles, engineering ingenuity, and an uncompromising dedication to safety. By partnering with global giants and maintaining a robust in-house team of structural engineers, naval architects, and certified QA/QC professionals, we help shape the region's industrial infrastructure.

Our Vision

To grow into a leading consortium in offshore engineering, known for technical competence, contributing to the socio-economic advancement of the Sultanate of Oman.

Our Mission

To offer highly safe, value-driven, and cost-effective fabrication and staffing services that keep our clients' operations running efficiently and seamlessly.

Certified Technical Staffing

Red Hawk Investment provides highly qualified and pre-vetted technical manpower on contract bases (short-term, long-term, or project-specific). Our workforce is backed by experience at top-tier shipyards and marine fabricators.

QA/QC Engineers

Highly qualified and certified QA/QC professionals to inspect weld procedures, structural tolerances, and documentation.

ASME/AWS Welders

Welders certified in GTAW, SMAW, FCAW, Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Inconel, and Duplex/Super Duplex processes.

Structural Fitters

Experienced professionals capable of reading complex engineering shop drawings to align structural steel and pipes.

NDT Inspectors

Technicians certified to ASNT Level II or CSWIP for UT Scanning, UT Gauging, MPT, Radioactive, and Eddy Current testing.

Structural Fabricators

Skilled fabricators capable of cutting, rolling, shaping, bending, and hammering metals to close tolerances.

NACE Coating Inspectors

NACE Level 2 certified painters and coating inspectors ensuring anti-corrosion protection in high-salinity marine environments.

Safety & Compliance

HSE & Quality Management Policy

In offshore fabrication and heavy erection, safety is not a statistic—it is our absolute operating standard. Red Hawk is committed to protecting our personnel, our clients, and the environment.

  • 1

    Dedicated to protecting, preserving, and cleaning the working environment in a responsible manner.

  • 2

    Mitigate operational risks through safe working conditions and continual improvement in operational safety.

  • 3

    Daily job hazard analysis (JHA) meetings where team members evaluate risks before tasks begin.

  • 4

    Routine training and development programs to align crew with the best international safety practices.

  • 5

    Extensive onboarding safety training for all new team members before active deployment.

  • 6

    Incentivizing and rewarding workers for demonstrating safe, proactive, and compliant operations.
